Every quarter, forever

We keep looking at it so you don't have to.

Every three months you get a one-page inspection of your site. Plain English. What's working, what's broken, what your competitors did that you haven't.

It's included with every Rework, and it doesn't stop. Not a subscription, not a trial. As long as the site is up, we inspect it.

Fix it yourself, or have us do it. Every item comes with what it would take. Small fixes are a flat fee. Bigger moves — new pages, new markets, getting found — are a conversation.

Why free? Because a site that's right for two years and wrong for the next eight isn't fixed. And because we'd rather earn the next job than bill for the check-up.

Straight answers

What people ask on the call.

Two days? Really?

Yes. The clock starts when the first call ends. It works because we don't wait on you: we write the copy, we find the photos, and you review instead of produce. If you want to supply your own copy and photos, you can, but it isn't required and it doesn't change the price.

What if I want more changes than two rounds?

Extra rounds are a flat $250 each. In practice almost nobody needs a third. If we got it that wrong on the call, that's on us and we'll say so.

Who owns the site?

You do. The domain stays in your name, the site files are yours, and if you ever want to leave we hand everything over. No hostage situations.

What's the catch on the "forever" inspection?

Only that the site has to still be up and we have to be able to see it. If you move it somewhere we can't reach or the domain lapses, we can't inspect it. That's the whole caveat.

We already have a site. Can you just fix it?

Usually it's faster and cheaper to rebuild than to repair a site from a decade ago, which is why the price is the price. If yours is recent and mostly good, we'll tell you on the call and point you somewhere else.

Is this a template?

It's a structure that works for industrial companies, filled with your equipment, your capabilities, your photos, your people. Your competitors won't have the same site because they don't have the same shop.

I'm an advisor. What if the deal takes three years, or never closes?

Nothing changes for your client. They got a working site for $2,000 and a free inspection every quarter. The Transfer Packet is ready whenever it's needed, and if it's never needed, they still have a site that's right. There's no clock on our end.

Can the buyer keep using you after close?

Yes, and they usually do. Same site, same inspection, same maintenance option. The acquirer inherits a known quantity instead of a mystery, and we're the people who already know where everything is.
