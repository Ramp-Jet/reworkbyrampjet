Why this exists
The lead came in Tuesday. Somebody saw it Friday.

Nobody decided to ignore it. The form goes to an inbox somebody watches when things are quiet, the quote in front of them was worth more, and by the time anyone looks, the buyer has already called someone else.

The fix isn't trying harder. It's that following up stops being a thing a person remembers to do and becomes a thing the system does on a schedule — with a record of who touched what, so nothing sits unowned.

Straight answers

What people ask on the call.

Do you replace my CRM?

No. If you have one, we write into it. If you don't, you don't need to buy one for this — the log we keep is yours and exports whenever you want it.

Who does the follow-up, you or us?

The system sends what's scheduled; your people handle anything that needs a human. You'll never wonder which is which, because every touch is logged against a name.

What counts as an inquiry?

A form, an email, a phone call, or a walk-in somebody logs. If a person asked you for something, it counts.

What happens to a lead nobody wants?

It gets marked dead with a reason, and it stays in the log. Dead is a decision somebody made, not a thing that happened because everyone was busy.

Can I turn it off?

Any month. The log, the history and the contacts are yours on the way out — we'll send them without being asked.
